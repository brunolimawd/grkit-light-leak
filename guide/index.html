Light Leak field guide

Pick a style, decide where light enters the frame and set how it changes from one shot to the next.

Take your first shot

  1. Open ADJ → GRK → Light Leak and choose one of the six styles. The default style is Off.
  2. Press Fn for Strength, Leak Position and Variation. To match the site's examples, try Medium strength, Original position and Variation 1.
  3. Take a photo and check the JPEG in playback. The viewfinder icon shows which style is active, not the effect itself.

The six styles

These descriptions use Variation 1. In every style you can change strength, entry side and variation.

Slit Flare
White beam, red curtain and cyan reflection. Original entry: right.
Burnt Edge
Irregular bright seam with red-orange spill. Original entry: right.
Film Band
Raised gold band with a red lip and cool fringe. Original entry: bottom.
Amber Veil
Amber pool with a diagonal red tail. Original entry: top.
Prismatic Arc
Red, gold, teal and blue arc. Original entry: right.
Color Fog
Diagonal gold, teal, blue and rose wash. Original entry: top.

What the controls do

Strength
Weak, Medium or Strong. Medium is the default.
Leak Position
Right, Left, Top, Bottom, Random or Original. Original uses the default entry side of the selected style. Directions are tied to the sensor and rotate with portrait shots.
Variation
1 is the original shape and palette; 2 and 3 change both. Auto is the default and chooses a variation per shot without changing the selected style, strength or side.
Random style
Draws a style, strength and side per shot without changing your saved choices. It uses the fixed variation if you selected one or draws a variation if set to Auto. Consecutive shots may repeat.

The main JPEG and its previews use the same draw. In monochrome, Light Leak adds brightness without tint.

Viewfinder icon

With Show Icon on, the symbol appears on the left of the still viewfinder in full-information DISP. One, two or three white segments indicate Weak, Medium or Strong; Random shows a die with no segments. The icon is never saved into the image.

The camera's native grid can be hidden while the icon is visible. Turn Fn → Show Icon → Off to restore the previous grid behavior, or use GRKit Guides.

Frequently asked questions

Does Light Leak change RAW files? #

No. The effect is applied to the JPEG and its previews, not the raw sensor data.

Can I preview the effect before shooting? #

No. Light Leak has no live-view effect preview. The viewfinder icon indicates the selected style, and strength when it is known before capture.

Does it work on the GR IIIx or GR IV? #

Those cameras have not been verified on hardware for this module. The tested combination is RICOH GR III with firmware 2.10. See the compatibility notes.

Is the effect saved in Recipes? #

No. Light Leak settings are not stored in GRKit Recipes.

How does it interact with other effects? #

Light Leak is applied after the color look and Glow, and before Film-like Grain and Date Imprint. It does not tint the added border.

Where does LITLEAK.GRM go? #

Copy it to GRKIT/MODULES/LITLEAK.GRM from the root of the SD card. See the installation steps for details.
